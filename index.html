Three60 Cricket Academy

Three60 means every angle.

A great cover drive only gets you so far. Our coaches look at the whole player, and every session works on a different side of your game, so you improve all the way round.

90°

Questions

Before you come.

Anything else, just ask on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

Where exactly is Aark Arena?

In Sevasi, Vadodara, opposite Aark International School (Gujarat 391101). The map at the bottom of this page opens directions on your phone.

Can we play when it rains?

Yes. The nets are inside a covered hall with floodlights, so the monsoon and the evenings do not stop practice.

Can my child join the academy?

Yes, young players train here every week. Message us your child's age and level, and we'll suggest the right batch and timing.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you book: a net session, bowling machine time, or an academy batch. Ask on WhatsApp and you get the current price straight away.

What should I bring?

Your own bat, pads and gloves if you have them, plus water and shoes suited to turf. If you are new and missing kit, ask us first.
